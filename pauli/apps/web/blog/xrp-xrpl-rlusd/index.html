FIELD NOTE 006 · XRPL

Four things people keep mixing together.

Ripple, XRPL, XRP and RLUSD are related. They are not interchangeable.

The clean version.

Ripple

A technology company building payment and digital-asset products.

XRPL

The XRP Ledger: an open-source distributed ledger/network.

XRP

The native asset of XRPL. It can be used for transaction fees, liquidity and bridging value inside the ledger's ecosystem.

RLUSD

Ripple's U.S.-dollar-backed stablecoin, designed to maintain a one-dollar value and issued on XRPL, Ethereum and other supported networks.

Why the distinction matters.

If Ripple succeeds as a company, that does not automatically mean every transaction must use XRP. If XRPL grows, different assets can still move across it. RLUSD can serve stable-dollar settlement while XRP serves a different role.

Infrastructure, native asset and stablecoin are different layers. MaxPay should evaluate each layer by what it actually does rather than by token narratives.

What MaxPay takes from this.

  • Keep XRPL as a potential settlement adapter.
  • Treat XRP and RLUSD as separate asset options.
  • Do not make price predictions part of the product.
  • Measure speed, fees, liquidity, custody and operational risk.
  • Use the rail only when it is genuinely better for the payment being made.
