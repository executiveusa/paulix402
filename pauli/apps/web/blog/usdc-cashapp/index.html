FIELD NOTE 007 · PRACTICAL RAILS

Use the familiar rail. Own the fallback.

MaxPay should not force customers to care about payment ideology. It should let the business choose the right route.

Why start with USDC.

For an internal team pilot, USDC is useful because the amount stays understandable in dollar terms while settlement can be verified onchain. That makes it a strong first programmable rail for proving MaxPay's request → verify → ledger → receipt flow.

Why keep Cash App.

Cash App is already familiar to U.S. customers. Cash App Business Accounts can receive payments for goods and services and deposit those payments, less applicable fees, into the business's Cash App balance.

But Cash App is not the sovereign core. Business accounts remain subject to identity/business verification, Block's terms, reserves/chargebacks and account controls. We treat it as a convenience adapter—not as the only place the business can get paid.

The MaxPay model.

One request. Several eligible rails. One internal receipt format.

  • USDC for direct programmable settlement.
  • Cash App for U.S. customer familiarity.
  • x402 for machine payments.
  • Additional rails only after they pass verification and replay-safety tests.

The point is not to remove every traditional provider. The point is to prevent any one provider from becoming the entire business.
